# The Elements of Quantitative Investing: reference copy

The complete user-supplied [eqi.pdf](eqi.pdf) is preserved unchanged here.
Its title page identifies Giuseppe A. Paleologo and the draft date **2024-07-10**.
The PDF has 376 pages; its file modification date is not its edition date.

The [manifest](manifest.json) records provenance, byte counts and SHA-256
checksums. The primary PDF checksum is
`ffb35c7854fb308af5e17ef8137941fbd62270a0440d93d3eb7827437717c686`.

Two publisher downloads provide supplementary edition information:

- [Wiley contents](wiley-contents.pdf), downloaded from the

- [Wiley chapter 1 excerpt](wiley-chapter-1-excerpt.pdf), downloaded from the

These supplements belong to the 2025 published edition. They do not replace
the draft or establish that its unfinished sections were completed in the same
way. The draft has 13 substantive chapters and a chapter 14 appendix; the
published edition has a different structure and numbering.
