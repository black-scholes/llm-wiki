# LLM Wiki

This is the home page for a practical guide to large language models: what
they learn, how they are trained, how they are evaluated, and how they are
used in real systems.

The wiki favours short, connected notes. Each page should answer a specific
question and make its assumptions visible.

## Browse by topic

- [Foundations](foundations/) - the core concepts behind language models
- [Systems](systems/) - the infrastructure around training and inference
- [Techniques](techniques/) - methods for improving model behaviour and use
- [Paper notes](papers/) - focused summaries with source links
- [Glossary](glossary/) - quick definitions and links to deeper pages

## Suggested reading path

1. Start with tokenisation, embeddings, and the transformer architecture.
2. Follow the training path through objectives, optimisation, and data.
3. Continue with inference, evaluation, and deployment constraints.
4. Use the technique and paper sections for deeper or more current topics.

The sections are placeholders for the first content pass and may evolve as
the wiki develops.
